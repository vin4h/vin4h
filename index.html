About

Over eight years of backend and full-stack engineering, most of it spent inside distributed systems — message queues, event-driven services, and the APIs that connect them.

I've worked across agribusiness, fintech, and communications platforms: currently at ILP FARM building agricultural and financial management software, and previously at Nubank and Sinch, working with Clojure, Kafka, Elixir, and Node.js.

Before that, I taught software engineering and web development at IFMS — which is probably why I still enjoy explaining a system as much as building one.
